03 / Press play

  1. Download client launcher after Generate. Open the downloaded Depth-game.luau as text.
  2. In a fresh Roblox client session, run the entire file with your client script loader. This launches Depth with your ROM already loaded. Do not launch the empty Depth script first.
  3. The loader must support game:HttpGet and loadstring. The launcher fetches Depth's runtime from GitHub; your ROM stays in the local script. Large files may exceed your loader's limits.
  4. Small ROM alternative: Copy base64, then Depth → Library → Load pasted ROM. The paste box supports at most 200,000 base64 characters (about 146 KiB of ROM data).
  5. Studio / Rojo: use the exports above to install ReplicatedStorage.Assets.ROM in your own place. Export battery saves before replacing a game.
Use a GBA homebrew ROM or a backup you have permission to use. Extract ZIP files first. Compatibility is incomplete and some games run slowly. Keep generated files private: they contain your ROM.

Whose ROM is it?

Yours stays in your browser tab. Each visitor chooses a file from their own device; there is no shared ROM library, account lookup, or upload.

The downloaded launcher contains only the ROM you selected. Reloading the page clears its selection; downloaded files remain on your device. To play on another device, transfer your launcher yourself. GitHub serves this page and may log normal page requests; the helper does not send it your ROM.
